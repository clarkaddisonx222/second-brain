# Psalm 4 — In Peace I Will Lie Down and Sleep

**Key:** G Major | **Tempo:** ~60 BPM | **Time:** 4/4

---

## Intro
**G  D/F#  Em  C** (2x)

---

## Verse 1
**G**              **D/F#**             **Em**
Answer me when I call to you,
          **C**              **G**
my righteous God.
       **D/F#**           **Em**
Give me relief from my distress;
          **C**         **D**
have mercy on me and hear my prayer.

          **C**              **G**

       **D/F#**           **Em**

    **C**       **D**

## Verse 3
**G**              **D/F#**             **Em**

          **C**              **G**
when you are on your beds,
     **D/F#**              **Em**

       **C**              **D**

    **G**

## Verse 4

          **C**              **G**

       **C**       **D**
